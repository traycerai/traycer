This page covers **IDE extension** pricing (credit-based, model rate card). Using Traycer Desktop? See [Desktop Pricing](/account/pricing).

## Plans & Billing

Traycer has two plans. Individuals use Traycer for free; teams subscribe to Sync.

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|-------|------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | Traycer with your own coding agents, AI planning and task tracking, unlimited agent sessions, remote hosts, the mobile app. No token metering. |
| **Sync** | $20 per user / month, or $16 per user / month billed yearly | Everything in Free for your whole team, plus Cloud Sync, device switching and team collaboration. |

**Yearly billing is 20% off.**

Neither plan includes monthly credits. Inference through the Traycer provider is pay as you go: buy a credit bundle when you need one (see [Bundles](#bundles)).

If you subscribed to an earlier plan (Lite, Pro, Ultra or Ultra+), you keep it for as long as you stay subscribed, on its original terms: the credits it includes renew every monthly billing cycle and do not roll over. Those plans can no longer be bought or switched to; you can cancel at any time.

### Trials
- Teams start Sync with a 7-day free trial.
- No credit card required to start the trial.

<Accordion title="Can I attach my card during the trial to avoid any interruption?" defaultOpen>
  Yes. You can add a payment method at any time during your trial on the [platform](https://platform.traycer.ai/). Your subscription will continue automatically when the trial ends.
</Accordion>

## Usage & Limits

Traycer uses **credits** to meter usage across artifacts and chat.

### Key Terms
- **Credit:** The billing unit used for usage.
- **Bundle credits:** Additional credits you can buy separately, starting at $10 bundles.
- **Rate card:** Each action has a credit cost based on the operation type.

### Rate Card

<Info>
  Action costs vary based on your selected model profile and reasoning level. Use our interactive pricing calculator to estimate costs for your workflow.

  **[View Pricing Calculator →](/extension/tasks/models#pricing-calculator)**
</Info>

### Model Profile Multipliers

The pricing calculator shows the base action cost. Final cost per action can change based on selected model profile and reasoning level.

- Use **Balanced** for default cost/performance.
- Use **Frontier** for higher quality and deeper reasoning.
- Use a **Custom profile** to override specific steps.

See [Model Profiles & Cost Multipliers](/extension/tasks/models) for:

- Step-level profile defaults
- Reasoning effort multipliers
- Cost multiplier formula used for estimation

<Note>
If any model call in a request exceeds 200K input tokens, the request cost is multiplied by 2x. This applies to every model.
</Note>

### Bundles

Inference through the Traycer provider is paid for with credit bundles.

- Bundles are free-form amounts starting at **$10**.
- The amount you buy is added to your credit balance at checkout.

### FAQ

<Accordion title="Do team members share credits?">
  No. Credits are allocated per seat by default. Enterprise plans provide centralized billing and enforce privacy mode.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do unused credits expire?">
  Bundle credits never expire. Credits included in an earlier plan (Lite, Pro, Ultra, Ultra+) reset at the end of each monthly billing cycle and do not carry over.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my artifacts if I downgrade?">
  All previously generated artifacts remain accessible permanently, regardless of your plan tier.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are Enterprise plans?">
  Enterprise teams are on the Sync plan, priced per seat like any team. It includes:
  - **Centralized Billing** - All team members are billed under a single invoice for easier expense tracking.
  - **Seat Management** - Invite, remove, and manage members from a central dashboard.
  - **Centralized Remote MCP Connections** - Connect and manage servers at the organization level for all team members.
  - **Org-Wide Privacy Mode** - Enforced by default to protect sensitive data.
  - **Dedicated Support Channel** - A private Slack or Discord channel for faster communication and priority support.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does deleting tasks or artifacts refund my credits?">
  No. Deleting previous tasks, plans, or any generated artifacts does not refund consumed credits.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if artifact generation fails?">
  If an artifact generation fails, we will automatically refund the consumed credits. In case your credits were not refunded, please reach out to us for assistance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do plan changes work?">
  - **Teams** move between Free and Sync from the team settings page. Cancelling Sync takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle, with no loss of access until then.
  - **New individual accounts** are on Free; there is nothing to upgrade to. Credits are bought as bundles when needed.
  - **Earlier plans** (Lite, Pro, Ultra, Ultra+) are kept by their existing subscribers and can be cancelled but not changed. All generated artifacts remain available permanently.
</Accordion>
