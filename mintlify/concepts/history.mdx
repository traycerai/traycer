History is the place to find Tasks you have worked on before, and the messages inside them. Press **⌘Y / Ctrl+Y** to open it.

Open History when you want to return to earlier work, find a shared Task, organize Tasks with labels and groups, or clean up Tasks you no longer need.

## What History Shows

Each row is a Task. From left to right, a row shows:

- An icon that shows when the Task's agents are working or need your attention.
- The Task's own icon and color, if you set a [Task appearance](#labels-groups-and-appearance).
- The title, with an **Open** badge when the Task is already open in a tab.
- The Task's group and up to two labels (one when it's in a group), with "+N" for the rest.
- When it was last updated. Hovering a row whose worktrees have pull requests swaps the time for PR pills.

Hovering a row also reveals its actions: pin, rename, [Sweep](/concepts/sweep) ("Sweep this task's worktrees"), and delete.

Opening a row opens that Task.

## Search

The search box looks through Tasks and the messages inside them. Three tabs choose what you see, each with a count while you're searching:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Matching Tasks, then matching messages. |
| **Tasks** | Only Tasks. The box reads "Search by title, repo, branch, or PR". |
| **Messages** | Only chats with matching messages. The box names the host it searches. |

Task search matches:

- The Task's title and repositories.
- The branch of any of its worktrees, and its pull request number (`84`, `#84`, or `PR #84`).
- A worktree's folder name, or its full path when the query contains `/`.

Branch, pull request, and worktree matches come only from worktrees on the active host.

Message search looks through the chats stored on the active host, across all your Tasks, and needs at least 2 characters. Click a result to open that chat at the message. History's filters don't apply to messages; when a filter is on, the Messages section says "Filters apply to tasks only." Use **Refine in chat search** to continue in [Search chats](/concepts/search-and-navigation).

Press **Esc** to clear the search. The arrow keys move from the search box into the results and back.

## Filter And Sort

Click **Filter** to narrow the Task list:

| Filter | What it matches |
|---|---|
| **Ownership** | **Mine** or **Shared** Tasks. |
| **Labels** | Tasks with the labels you pick. |
| **Groups** | Tasks in the groups you pick, or with **No group**. |
| **Repositories** | Tasks linked to the repositories you pick. |
| **Hosts** | Tasks whose agents ran on the hosts you pick. |
| **Workspaces** | Tasks linked to the workspace folders you pick. |

When you pick more than one label, repository, host, or workspace, an **OR** / **AND** toggle appears next to that filter: **OR** matches Tasks with any of them, **AND** only Tasks with all of them. **Clear** removes every filter.

The sort menu orders Tasks by **Most recent**, **Last viewed**, **Relevance**, **Oldest**, **Title A → Z**, or **Title Z → A**. Typing a search switches to **Relevance** unless you picked a sort yourself.

### Pin Tasks

Hover a row and click its pin icon to pin the Task to the top. Pinned Tasks stay above the others in every sort. Click the pin again to unpin it.

Pinning needs a confirmed sign-in, and some Tasks can't be pinned; the pin's tooltip explains why.

## Labels, Groups, And Appearance

You can organize Tasks with labels, groups, and a personal icon and color. Right-click a row to reach them:

| Menu item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Labels** | Opens "Task organization", where you add or remove your labels, create a label, or copy someone else's label to your own list. |
| **Task appearance** | Sets an **Icon** (an emoji or initials) and a **Color** for the Task. |
| **Add to group** / **Move to group** | Puts the Task in one of your groups, or a **New group…**. |
| **Remove from group** | Takes the Task out of its group. |

Clicking a row's group or label chips also opens "Task organization". To rename, recolor, or delete your labels across every Task, open the **⋯** menu in the History toolbar and choose **Manage Labels**.

Who sees what:

| What | Visible to |
|---|---|
| **Labels** | Everyone with access to the Task ("Labels are visible to everyone with access to this task."). You can only add your own labels; viewers can inspect labels and copy them. |
| **Groups** | Only you. A Task can be in one of your groups at a time, and a group with no Tasks left disappears. |
| **Task appearance** | Only you. |

**Imported** and **Automation** are system labels that Traycer adds. They're marked "System" and can't be edited or removed.

Labels, groups, and appearance need a confirmed sign-in. Changes that haven't reached the cloud yet show "Waiting to sync", or "Sign in to sync your changes" when you need to sign in again.

## Open And Manage Tasks

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Click the row. |
| **Open in Background** | Right-click the row, or middle-click it. Opens the Task in a tab without switching to it. |
| **Open in New Window** | Right-click the row. |
| **Rename** | Hover the row and click the pencil next to the title. |
| **Select** | Click **Select** in the toolbar, or Cmd/Ctrl-click a row. **Select all**, **Deselect all**, and **Cancel** work on the selection. |
| **Sweep Worktrees…** | Right-click a row, or use the row's paintbrush. With a selection, the toolbar's paintbrush sweeps all selected Tasks. See [Sweep](/concepts/sweep). |
| **Delete** | Hover a row and click its delete button, or select Tasks and click delete in the toolbar. |
| **Refresh** | Reloads the History list. |

Viewers of a shared Task can't rename or delete it. Renaming, deleting, and pinning also need a confirmed sign-in.

### Delete Tasks

Deleting asks for confirmation and can't be undone. If the Tasks you're deleting have worktrees on the active host that no other Task uses, the confirmation adds **Remove local worktrees too?**, listing each worktree with anything that would be lost, such as uncommitted changes or commits not on the default branch. Worktrees proven safe to remove are checked for you; check or uncheck the rest.

Checked worktrees are removed the same way [Sweep](/concepts/sweep) removes them, including their teardown script. Unchecked worktrees stay on disk; find them later in [Settings › Worktrees](/settings/worktrees).

## What History Is Not

History is not the same as the **Agents** panel.

- Use **History** to find and open Tasks.
- Use [Agents](/panels/agents) to continue an agent inside an open Task.
- Use [Artifacts](/panels/artifacts) to find specs, tickets, stories, and reviews inside a Task.

History helps you get back to the right Task. The Task's panels hold the actual work surfaces and documents.
