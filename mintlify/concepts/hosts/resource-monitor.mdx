Click **Resources** in the app header to open the resource monitor. It shows CPU, resident memory, and RAM share totals alongside agents and their running work.

## Find Running Work

Use search to narrow the list. Sort by **Memory**, **CPU**, **Name**, or **Tab order**. Expand an agent to inspect its spawned shells and operating-system processes.

## Stop Selected Work

Enter selection mode and check the rows you want to act on. The action label reflects the selection, such as **Stop 1 · Kill 1** for a shell and agent. Review that label before clicking: the action ends selected work.
