A `mermaid` code block in an artifact renders as an inline diagram. Ask an agent to include a Mermaid diagram when a flow or relationship is easier to understand visually.

## Inspect A Diagram

Use **Edit source** to inspect or change its Mermaid text, and **Close source** to return to the diagram. Click the rendered diagram itself to open fullscreen. Use the zoom controls and drag to pan; **Download PNG** exports the diagram as an image.
