title: "Sharing"
description: "Invite people to a Task, share it with a team, and choose which of your agents collaborators see."
---

The **Sharing** panel shows who has access to the Task and lets you invite people and share the Task with a team.

The top of the panel reads "Updated" with the time the list was last loaded. Click **Refresh** to load it again.

## Roles

Everyone with access to a Task has one of two roles:

- **Owner**: the person who created the Task. The Owner role can't be given to someone else from the panel.
- **Editor**: everyone else with access, whether invited directly or through a team.

Anyone who can open a Task can work in it, including keeping its artifacts as files on disk and inviting more people. Only the Owner can remove people and share the Task with a team.

For the files on disk, see [Artifacts on Disk](/panels/artifacts#artifacts-on-disk).

## Invite People

Everyone with access sees an invite box at the top of the panel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add people">
    Type an email address or GitHub handle into "Enter email or GitHub handle" and click **Add**, or press Enter. Repeat to queue several people.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Invite 1 person**, or **Invite N people** for several.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everyone you invite becomes an Editor.

People invited by GitHub handle may not get an email about it.

## People With Access

**People with access** lists everyone who has direct access to the Task, with each person's role.

The Owner can remove a person with the remove button on their row. Removing someone ends their access right away and closes any sessions they have open on the Task.

A Task always keeps an Owner: the last Owner can't be removed.

Editors see the list and each person's role.

## Teams

The **Teams** section lists the teams the Task is shared with, and, for the Owner, the teams it could be shared with. Only the Owner can share with a team.

1. Find the team under **Teams**.
2. Click **Share**.

Everyone on the team becomes an Editor. The Owner can later remove the team, which ends access for everyone who had it through the team.

The team must be on the Sync plan. See [Share Tasks With a Team](/account/teams#share-tasks-with-a-team).

## Share My Agents

**Share my agents** controls whether collaborators can see the agent chats you run in this Task. Collaborators can view and clone a shared chat, but they can never act in it.

- Turning it on asks "Share your agents with this task?" and shares your existing and future agent chats in the Task.
- Turning it off asks "Make your agents private?" and makes all of your agents in the Task private, including future ones.

To override it for one agent, open that agent's row menu in the [Agents](/panels/agents) panel and choose **Share with task** or **Make private**.

Terminal agents are never shared.

## Requirements

- **Sign in.** Sharing works on your Traycer account. If your sign-in can't be confirmed, the panel says access can't be checked until you sign in again.
- **The Task must be in the cloud.** Collaborators open the cloud copy of the Task. Cloud sync is included for every signed-in account, and new Tasks upload automatically. If you invite someone before the upload finishes, Traycer waits briefly; if it still hasn't finished, try again once the Task has synced. See [Sync and offline](/concepts/sync-and-offline).
- **Plans.** Inviting individual people works on any plan. Sharing with a team needs the team to be on the Sync plan.

There is no link sharing. People get access only by invitation or through a team.

## What Collaborators See

- The Task's artifacts, with their comment threads.
- The agent chats that the Task's members share. See [Share My Agents](#share-my-agents).
