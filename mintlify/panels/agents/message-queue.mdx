While an agent is running on the Chat interface, press **Enter** to queue a follow-up. Queued messages appear in the **Message Queue** above the composer. When the current turn ends, the next message is picked up automatically unless the queue is paused.

## Manage Queued Messages

- Drag a row by its grip to change its position.
- Choose **Edit** to load a queued message into the composer, change it, and press Enter to save. **Cancel** leaves the queued message as it was.
- Choose **Delete** to remove a message from the queue.
- Use **Pause** to hold the queue and **Resume** to continue processing it.

## Steer The Current Turn

When steering is available, choose the row's steer action to send that message into the running turn. The row can show **Waiting for steer** before it becomes **Steering**. You can also use the composer's displayed steering shortcut, **Cmd+Enter** on macOS.
