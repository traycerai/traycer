An agent can ask structured questions while it works. A question card appears in place of the Chat composer, with a pager when there is more than one question.

## Answer Questions

Choose an option for a single-choice question to advance automatically. For a multiple-choice question, select your options and click **Next**. Use **Other** to enter your own answer, then **Submit** to send your answers. **Skip** dismisses the request.

After submission, the composer returns and a summary such as **Answered 3 questions** remains in the transcript. Expand it to review your submitted answers.

## Compare Answers In A Fork

The question card offers two fork actions. Each opens the **Fork agent** dialog:

- **Cross Question** creates a side conversation with the question carried as a review card. The composer is free for a follow-up question; the carried card is not an answer request in the new chat.
- **A/B Fork** creates a conversation with the questions pending again, so you can try another answer.

Both forks carry history up to the question checkpoint. The original conversation remains available for comparison. Where the fork opens depends on your tile placement setting.
