The composer is where you write the next message on the Chat interface. Pick a [coding agent and model](/agents-and-models/coding-agents#models) and [permissions](/panels/agents#permissions) before sending.

## Mention Context

Type `@` to open the mention picker. Browse a category or type to search across files, folders, agents, terminals, and artifacts. Select a result to insert an inline mention and continue writing. You can include several kinds of context in one message.

## Stash A Prompt

In the landing composer, press **Cmd+S** on macOS or **Ctrl+S** on Windows and Linux to stash a draft. The composer clears and the **Stash** count increases. Open **Stashed prompts** and choose **Insert** to bring a saved prompt back into the composer, or **Delete** to remove it.


## Quote A Reply

Select text in an assistant reply and click **Quote**. The selection becomes a blockquote in the composer, with space below it for your follow-up.

## Context Usage

When usage is available, the context chip shows how much of the conversation's context window remains. Click it to inspect the token breakdown. The separate **Compact** button beside the chip summarizes the conversation to free space; progress and the resulting token counts appear in the transcript.
