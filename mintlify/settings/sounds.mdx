**Sounds** chooses the chime for each kind of notification. It sits in the **Application** group of [Settings](/settings), so one set of chimes applies to notifications from every host.

Which events create a notification in the first place is set per host, in Host › [Notifications](/settings/notifications). How an alert reaches you, through your OS or your phone, is set in [Alerts](/settings/alerts). For what notifications are and where they appear, see [Notifications](/concepts/notifications).

## Chimes

Choose a sound for each kind of notification. Choosing a sound plays it, so you can try each one.

| Notification | Covers | Default sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Needs action** | Approvals and interviews. | Orbit |
| **Failure** | Errored turns, stalls, crashes, and rate limits. | Rift |
| **Done** | Completed or intentionally stopped turns. | Prism |
| **Info** | Sharing, comments, access changes, and other informational notifications. | Ember |

The sounds are Classic, Prism, Ripple, Ember, Orbit, Rift, Coin, Bloop, Cuckoo, Ta-da and Beacon. Choose **None** to keep that kind of notification silent.
