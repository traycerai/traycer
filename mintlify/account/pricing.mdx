This page covers **Traycer Desktop** pricing. Using the IDE extension? See [Extension Pricing](/extension/pricing).

Traycer pricing separates product access from inference usage.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Best for | Includes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Individuals | Traycer with your own coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Cursor, and more), AI planning and task tracking, unlimited agent sessions, [cloud sync](/concepts/sync-and-offline) across your devices, sharing Tasks with the people you invite, remote hosts, the [mobile app](/account/mobile-app), agent-to-agent communication. No token metering, no inference credits required. |
| **Sync** | \$20/user/month, or \$16/user/month billed yearly | Teams | Everything in Free for your whole team, plus sharing a Task with the whole team at once. Teams can start with a 7-day trial. |

Yearly billing saves 20%.

Cloud sync is included on every plan. The one thing that needs Sync is sharing a Task with a team: the team you share with must be on Sync. Sharing a Task with individual people works on every plan. See [Sharing](/panels/sharing) and [Share Tasks With a Team](/account/teams#share-tasks-with-a-team).

If you subscribed to an earlier plan (Lite, Pro, Ultra or Ultra+), you keep it for as long as you stay subscribed, on its original terms: the credits it includes renew every monthly billing cycle and do not roll over. Those plans can no longer be bought or switched to; you can cancel at any time.

## Trials

- **Teams.** A new team can try Sync free for 7 days. A team admin starts the trial with **Start Trial** on the team's **Billing** page in the [web dashboard](https://traycer.ai/billing). No card is needed to start it, and each team gets one trial. To keep Sync after the trial, add a payment method on the same page before it ends; otherwise the team moves to Free. See [Teams](/account/teams#seats-and-billing).
- **Individuals.** There is no trial for individuals. Free already includes cloud sync and everything else an individual uses.

## Inference Credits

Credits pay for models accessed through the **Traycer** provider. They are optional and pay as you go. Neither Free nor Sync includes monthly credits; only the earlier plans above do.

One credit is worth $1. To buy a bundle, open **Billing** in the [web dashboard](https://traycer.ai/billing) and click **Purchase Credits**:

- A bundle is a whole number of credits, at least 10.
- Bundle credits never expire.
- A personal account can hold at most 500 bundle credits, and a team at most 2,000. A purchase that would go over the limit is refused.

A team buys credits into one pool that its members share. See [Team credits](/account/teams#team-credits).

## What Costs Credits

Models available through the **Traycer** provider can consume inference credits. Credit usage is based on the upstream API pricing for the selected supported model, plus a **20% Traycer markup**.

There are no fixed per-request fees. Cost can still vary by operation, selected model, reasoning effort, and provider-side pricing.

Using other coding-agent paths, such as Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, or Cursor, does **not** consume Traycer credits and does **not** include a Traycer markup. Those paths use their own account, API, or provider billing outside Traycer. Use [Agents & Models](/agents-and-models/coding-agents) for coding-agent setup.

<Note>
**Auto mode is the exception.** When a conversation runs in Auto mode, a judge model reviews its commands. With the judge set to **Automatic**, Traycer's hosted model reviews them whenever Traycer inference is available, and that uses credits, even in a Claude Code or Codex conversation. **Settings → Permissions → Judge** shows which judge is active and how it is billed. To keep Auto mode off your credits, choose a specific judge model there. See [Judge](/settings/permissions#judge).
</Note>

## Refer & Earn

Open **Billing → Referrals** in the [web dashboard](https://traycer.ai/referrals) to get your invite link. When someone signs in through your link and then makes their first payment, such as buying a credit bundle, you each get 10 credits.

- The link works only for people who have never paid for Traycer.
- You can refer up to 100 people.

## Team Billing

A team on Sync is billed per seat, with one subscription for the whole team and one credit pool that its members share. See [Teams](/account/teams).
