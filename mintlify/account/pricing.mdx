This page covers **Traycer Desktop** pricing. Using the IDE extension? See [Extension Pricing](/extension/pricing).

Traycer pricing separates product access from inference usage.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Best for | Includes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Individuals | Traycer with your own coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Cursor, and more), AI planning and task tracking, unlimited agent sessions, remote hosts, the mobile app, agent-to-agent communication. No token metering, no inference credits required. |
| **Sync** | $20/user/month, or $16/user/month billed yearly | Teams | Everything in Free for your whole team, plus Cloud Sync, device switching and team collaboration. Teams start with a 7-day trial. |

Yearly billing saves 20%.

If you subscribed to an earlier plan (Lite, Pro, Ultra or Ultra+), you keep it for as long as you stay subscribed, on its original terms: the credits it includes renew every monthly billing cycle and do not roll over. Those plans can no longer be bought or switched to; you can cancel at any time.

## Inference Credits

Credits are used only for models accessed through the **Traycer** provider. They are optional and pay as you go: buy a credit bundle of any amount from $10 whenever you need one. Bundle credits never expire. Neither Free nor Sync includes monthly credits; only the earlier plans above do.

## What Costs Credits

Models available through the **Traycer** provider can consume inference credits. Credit usage is based on the upstream API pricing for the selected supported model, plus a **20% Traycer markup**.

There are no fixed per-request fees. Cost can still vary by operation, selected model, reasoning effort, and provider-side pricing.

Using other coding-agent paths, such as Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, or Cursor, does **not** consume Traycer credits and does **not** include a Traycer markup. Those paths use their own account, API, or provider billing outside Traycer. Use [Agents & Models](/agents-and-models/coding-agents) for coding-agent setup.

## Team Billing

Teams are billed per seat on the Sync plan, with centralized billing and seat management. Organization details live in [Organizations and Teams](/account/organizations).
