## How Traycer Works

- **Tasks** hold one piece of work: its agents, files, terminals and documents.
- **Agents** do the work. Talk to them in a Chat, or run their own CLI in a Terminal.
- **Artifacts** are the documents a Task keeps: specs, tickets, stories and reviews. They outlast any single conversation.
